# KutIt privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

KutIt is an audio trimmer for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## The short version

KutIt doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything happens on your phone.

## What KutIt does with your files

- When you choose a song or video, KutIt reads it from the file you picked, using the Android file picker. It makes a temporary working copy in the app's private cache so it can decode the audio.
- When you save, KutIt writes the trimmed audio to the place and name you choose in the Android save dialog.
- When you share, Android's share sheet sends the saved file to the app you pick. What that app does with it is governed by that app's own privacy policy.
- KutIt never changes or deletes your original file.
- Temporary working files stay in the app's private cache. They're replaced when you open another file, and they're removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## What KutIt does not do

- It has no internet permission, so it can't upload your files or send any data off your device.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking.
- It doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any files you didn't pick.

## Children

KutIt isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.
